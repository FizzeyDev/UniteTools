📖 How to Use - XP Calculator
1
Choose your Pokémon and set your starting level.
2
Set the Start Timer (when you start farming) of your session.
3
Configure the Enemy Team section - set the highest enemy Pokémon level to activate the Catch-Up Modifier if applicable.
4
Add kills from the Wild Pokémon grid. Click a Pokémon, set the timer and number of allies sharing XP, then confirm.
5
Add Player KO events via the Kill Queue panel - enter the victim's level, their streak, and whether you landed the killing blow.
6
Add Scoring events using the scoring quick-add row (timer + points scored).
7
Press Classic for an instant summary, or Advanced to watch a real-time simulation that you can speed up to x4.
💡
The XP Table at the bottom shows how much XP each wild Pokémon gives at every timer tick - use it to plan kills efficiently.

Passive XP: Automatic - 4 XP/sec between 10:00 and 8:00, then 6 XP/sec between 8:00 and 0:00.
XP Share: Coming Soon - will be implemented in a future update.
Catch-Up on Player KO: Unconfirmed - enable the option at your own risk to test the theoretical value.

⚠️ Disclaimer - Please read before using

This calculator is an estimation tool designed to approximate Pokémon UNITE's in-game mechanics as closely as possible. It does not claim to perfectly reproduce every match condition.

📊 Data Accuracy

XP values for wild Pokémon, passive XP, scoring, and KOs are based on in-game observations and measurements. They may differ slightly depending on the game version, balance updates, or undocumented conditions.

🎯 Partially Confirmed Mechanics

Some mechanics - in particular the application of the Catch-Up Modifier to Player KOs - are not officially documented by the developers and have not yet been exhaustively verified in-game. The corresponding values are theoretical extrapolations.

⚙️ Model Simplifications

This simulator does not reproduce every condition of a real match: Pokémon interactions, exact wild respawn timing, Exp. Share between nearby allies, held item buffs, and other situational factors are not yet integrated.

🔄 Ongoing Development

This tool is actively developed. Data is updated as patches are released. If you spot an inconsistency or have data to share, feel free to contribute.

This calculator is an independent community project, unaffiliated with TiMi Studio Group, Nintendo, or The Pokémon Company.
