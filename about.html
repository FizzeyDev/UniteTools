The creator

Who am I?

I'm Fizzey, a competitive Pokémon Unite player and passionate web developer. An active member of the French esport scene, I created Unite Tools to fill the gap in quality tools for our community.

My goal: make competitive resources accessible to everyone in the same place, for free, without compromising on quality.

"My goal is to create a tool for competitive community that I and my team will also use."
💡
The origin

Why Unite Tools?

The competitive Pokémon Unite scene was sorely lacking dedicated tools. No updated online draft simulator, no damage calculator, nothing to practice map control.

Unite Tools was born from that frustration. A solo project, built with passion, to raise the level of the entire community and beyond.
